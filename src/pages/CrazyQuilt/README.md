# 🧵 Crazy Quilt

A Crazy Quilt solitaire layout with exposed-card play, stock/waste flow, and dual suit foundations.

## Layout
- **Quilt Tableau:** 5 rows × 8 columns with alternating horizontal/vertical card orientation.
- **Stock:** Draw pile.
- **Waste:** Receives drawn cards and selected exposed quilt cards.
- **Foundations (8 total):**
  - 4 **up** foundations start at **Ace** and build up by suit.
  - 4 **down** foundations start at **King** and build down by suit.

## Core Rule
A quilt card is playable only when one of its **short sides** is exposed:
- Horizontal cards: left or right side open.
- Vertical cards: top or bottom side open.

## How to Play
1. Draw from **Stock** to **Waste**.
2. Select an exposed quilt card (or waste top card).
3. Move it to a legal foundation pile, or place an exposed quilt card onto the waste top.
4. Build each suit from both ends (A upward, K downward) until all non-starter cards are cleared.

## Win Condition
You win when no cards remain in quilt, stock, or waste.
